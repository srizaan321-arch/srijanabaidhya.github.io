About Srijana

Clinical care grounded in nutrition science.

Srijana Baidhya is a Clinical Dietitian with clinical, academic and public-health nutrition experience. Her practice combines patient-centred counselling with evidence-based dietary management.

She has worked in hospital clinical nutrition and has professional experience with nutrition projects and reproductive-health settings, including Star Hospital, JAFS Nutrition Project, National IVF Center for Reproductive Health and LG Clinic for Reproductive Health.

Her academic background includes a Bachelor in Nursing Science from Nobel Medical College, Kathmandu University, a Master's in Food and Nutrition from Tribhuvan University, and an ongoing PhD in Nutrition and Dietetics.
